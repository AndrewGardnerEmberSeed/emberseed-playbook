# EmberSeed AI Playbook

A free, clickable map for small and medium businesses: one quick win, the foundations, then AI in every part of
the business, in order. Every capability says what it is, why it's worth it (with sources), how to do it yourself,
the tools, the time and cost, and what to watch out for.

Live at https://andrewgardneremberseed.github.io/emberseed-playbook/ and https://emberseedautomation.com.au/playbook
